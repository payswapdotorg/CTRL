Session Watchdog how it works

How the watchdog keeps sessions alive

  • keep-going — a session whose turn ends without finishing gets the relaunch message ("continue" or your own) after a quiet grace window; failed sends retry, then the status becomes NEEDS INPUT.
  • freeze — a turn that stops moving (DOM and server-side) is reloaded, then moved to a fresh tab.
  • return — a tab that rolls back to the chat.z.ai home page is verified against the chats list, taken back to its session URL, and sent the relaunch message.
  • die — a session destroyed server-side gets bounded relaunch attempts, then a DEAD flag with a notification.
  • alarms — failures ring a looping siren (plus email / webhook / ntfy) until you silence them; relaunches chime once. Ubuntu: allow Chrome notifications in OS settings to hear the desk bell.
  • popups — capacity dialogs are dismissed with their Cancel button only; never their own suggestions, never captcha sliders.
  • auth — when the login dies you get a notification; signing in stays in your hands.
  • queued — sessions waiting for generation are left untouched (reloading them can wedge them).
  • diagnostics — "copy diagnostics" in the footer produces a redacted, paste-ready dump (version, settings, per-session verdicts and events).
